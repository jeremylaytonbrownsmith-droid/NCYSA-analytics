Families click through to clubs

Click tracking went live July 8. In roughly two weeks, 2,279 searches produced 2,314 clicks to club websites and emails — about one club visit per search. Coverage: good news

The tool quietly logs a coverage gap whenever a search finds no member club within 40 miles. Of 67 events recorded so far, nearly all trace to out-of-state visitors exploring the tool. Only 16 events from 14 NC-coded ZIPs appeared — several of them our own testing. After 8,000+ searches, genuine in-state gaps are rare, and the tool now gives NCYSA a permanent early-warning map for underserved areas.

A top-three page in one season

Find My Club drew 24,014 views from 19,255 users since January — the #3 page on ncsoccer.org by views, #2 by unique visitors, and #1 by a wide margin in key events (5,742). Only the homepage and Classic schedules see more traffic.
